Base meshes shipped with Marionette
===================================

hbm_female.glb, hbm_male.glb
  From "Human Base Meshes" v1.4.1 by Blender Studio (Blender Foundation),
  released under CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://studio.blender.org/
  The realistic female and male bodies (multires level 1, eyes included)
  were rigged for Marionette: joints placed from the mesh shape, bones named
  like Mixamo, automatic (bone heat) skin weights, quantised GLB.

  The two stylised bodies of the bundle are not included: their proportions
  (about 4.8 heads tall) read as childlike, and this extension offers anatomy
  and adult poses.
